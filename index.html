Vertex Mobile RP – Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 22, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Vertex Mobile RP (the “App”, package com.vertex.game), operated by Wasp Studio (“we”, “us”), collects, uses and shares information when you use the App. The App is a launcher and client that lets you download game files and connect to the Vertex roleplay multiplayer server.

By using the App you agree to this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the App.

1. Information We Collect

Account and gameplay information. When you log in or register, you provide a nickname and password to our login service. While you play, the game server processes the data needed to run the game, such as your in-game name, character data, chat messages, commands and gameplay actions.

Device and network information. The App and our servers receive technical data such as your IP address, device model, Android version, app version, language, and network state (for example Wi-Fi or mobile data). We use this to connect you to the server, prevent abuse and fix problems.

Usage and diagnostics data. We use Google Firebase services (Analytics, Cloud Messaging, Remote Config, Installations and Sessions) to understand how the App is used, to deliver notifications and to configure the App. Firebase may collect app interactions, session information, an app-instance identifier and diagnostic data. Crash reporting is switched off by default in the App.

Advertising ID. The App includes Google services that may access your device’s advertising ID for analytics and attribution. We do not show ads in the App. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings.

Google Sign-In. If a Google sign-in option is offered, Google shares basic profile information (such as your name and email address) with the App, subject to your consent.

Microphone audio. If you use in-game voice chat, the App accesses your microphone only while the feature is in use. Voice data is transmitted to other players on the server and is not recorded or stored by us unless stated otherwise in-game.

Files on your device. The App stores downloaded game files (the “cache”), settings and logs in your device storage.

2. Permissions the App Uses

  • Internet and network state – to log in, download game files and connect to the server.
  • Storage / all files access – to download, unpack and store the game cache (several gigabytes) and to read game files.
  • Microphone (RECORD_AUDIO) – for voice chat.
  • Bluetooth (BLUETOOTH, BLUETOOTH_CONNECT) – to use Bluetooth headsets and audio devices.
  • Notifications – to send you server news and updates.
  • Install packages – to let you install App updates that we provide.
  • Foreground service, wake lock, run at startup – to keep downloads and connections running reliably.
  • Vibration – for in-game feedback.

You can revoke optional permissions at any time in your device settings. Some features may stop working if you do.

3. How We Use Information

We use the information to:

  • provide, operate and maintain the App and the game server;
  • authenticate you and manage your account;
  • download and update game files;
  • send notifications and service messages;
  • monitor performance, understand usage and improve the App;
  • detect, prevent and investigate cheating, abuse, fraud and rule violations;
  • comply with legal obligations.

4. How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information. We share information only:

  • With other players – your in-game name, character, chat and voice are visible or audible to other players on the server.
  • With service providers – such as Google (Firebase) and our hosting and download providers, who process data on our behalf.
  • For legal reasons – if required by law, or to protect our rights, users or the public.
  • With your consent – in any other case, where you have agreed.

5. Data Retention

We keep account and gameplay data for as long as your account is active or as needed to run the game and enforce our rules. Analytics data is retained according to Firebase’s settings. When data is no longer needed, we delete or anonymize it.

6. Your Choices and Rights

You can:

  • turn off permissions in your device settings;
  • reset your advertising ID in Android settings;
  • uninstall the App to remove locally stored files;
  • request access to, correction of, or deletion of your account and data by contacting us (see Section 10).

Depending on where you live (for example the EEA, UK or California), you may have additional rights under local law, including the right to object to or restrict processing and to lodge a complaint with a data protection authority.

7. Account and Data Deletion

To request deletion of your account and associated data, email vrtx.xyz@gmail.com from the address linked to your account, or contact the server administrators through our official community channels, including your in-game nickname. We will process verified requests within a reasonable time, normally within 30 days. Some information may be kept where required for security, abuse prevention or legal reasons.

8. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security. Please choose a strong, unique password.

9. Children’s Privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.
